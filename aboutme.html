About me!

Hi! Some of my educational interests include Healthcare Technology and Human Computer Interaction! My goal in life is to find and build a career where I can be creative and design works that is meant to benefit and help others. I currently do a lot of community-based in the Southeast Asian community here at UCI and Orange County work which will be highlighted down below. Through my experience there I've learned about my passions across different profressions and I have helped establish a safe space for our community members. I've also dedicated some of my time through different projects highlighting different issues either in an educational setting or societal setting.

In my free time I enjoy spending it with my dog and picking up new hobbies!

Projects

Canvas Critter

This project was also for a previous course where I applied project management skills and other skills I learned from other classes to create a motivational extention for Canvas. We researched about productivity and different competitors who also have productivity extensions for Canvas.

Rideshare

Rideshare was a human-computer interaction project from a previous course. Our goal was to conduct HCI research to gauge how students use rideshare platforms and determine whether it was a social issue.

Nali

This was my first ever project in HTML and CSS which I did in my senior year of high school. This website was built as an online marketplace where people could sell clothing items and have functional login features. We did this because of the issues that fast fashion creates in the world and to encourage people to stop buying from fast fashion producers.

Leadership

Southeast Asian Student Association

Vice President

Part of this position includes planning general weekly meetings and socials for club members. Weekly general meeting consist of educational material surrounding various topics within Southeast Asia followed by an engaging activity to increase member satisfaction. I also help maintain and facilitate a safe community where everyone is understood and heard by all of our members. In addition to that, I also manage and keep track of the attendance count and membership for every meeting.

Retention Coordinator

In this position I managed the Southeast Asian Retention through Creating Hxstory (SEARCH) program. This program is a 12-week mentorship program between peers where upperclassmen pass down their knowledge to each other and their mentees on how to navigate college. We also highlight relevant issues that affect the Southeast Asian community and teach SEARCH members how to acknowledge and combat them. I really enjoyed being a coordinator for this program because it taught me a lot of valuable skills and I learned a lot about the community here at UCI.

Intern

As an intern for the club I helped the board members that I was shadowing with event planning and management. I also planned two general meetings for two of our programs alongside my co-intern and team of interns. We also worked on a two-quarter long archive project highlighting each of our clubs programs and curating an archive around photos taken throughout the year and providing metadata for them.
